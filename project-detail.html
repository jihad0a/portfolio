Clients

3 Catering Companies

Role

Digital Support Specialist

Timeline

Ongoing

Deliverables

Invoices, PDFs, Documents

The brief

Three UK-based catering companies were spending 8-10 hours per week on administrative and document work. Monthly invoices needed editing, PDF delivery notes required formatting, supplier images needed logo removal, and tax documents needed processing. They needed someone reliable to handle it all.

My mandate was simple: take over all digital document tasks and deliver every file clean, correct, and on time.

What I do

  • Invoice editing: Monthly invoice batches formatted and corrected for each client's branding.
  • PDF delivery notes: Clean, print-ready PDFs for every delivery, formatted consistently.
  • Image editing: Removing logos from supplier images, resizing photos for menus and social media.
  • Tax documents: Processing spreadsheets, formatting tax sheets, preparing client-ready files.
  • General document work: Menu updates, flyer preparation, document formatting.

"Jihad handles our monthly invoice editing and PDF documents reliably and quickly. Every file comes back clean and exactly right. He has saved us hours every week." — A. Rahman

Results

  • Time saved: 8-10 hours/week per client
  • Turnaround: Same-day delivery on most tasks
  • Error rate: Near-zero after verification step
  • Client retention: Ongoing monthly engagement
