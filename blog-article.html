What catering businesses should look for in a digital assistant

Not every business needs a full-time employee. Here is how to find the right person to handle your digital tasks.

Jihad

Web Developer & Catering Business Support

Why digital support matters for catering businesses

Catering businesses are moving fast — events, deliveries, menus, invoices, staff coordination. The one thing every owner I've worked with tells me is: the admin is the bottleneck.

From editing monthly invoices to formatting PDF delivery notes, removing logos from images, and managing tax spreadsheets — these tasks pile up fast.

What a good digital assistant actually does

  • Invoice editing. Monthly invoices need formatting, corrections, and client-specific customisation.
  • PDF and document work. Delivery notes, order confirmations, tax sheets, menu PDFs.
  • Image editing. Removing logos, resizing photos for menus and social media.
  • Website updates. Menu changes, pricing, adding new pages.
  • Admin panel management. Keeping booking systems and order dashboards in order.

What to look for when hiring digital support

  • Reliability over speed. Consistent delivery is worth more than fast but inconsistent work.
  • Attention to detail. A single error can cause confusion with a client.
  • Industry understanding. Someone who works with catering needs less hand-holding.
  • Communication. They tell you when it's done and ask when unclear.

Handling invoices, PDFs, and documents for catering businesses

From monthly invoices to delivery notes and tax sheets — my approach to keeping catering paperwork clean and on time.

Jihad

Web Developer & Catering Business Support

The document workload in catering

Catering businesses generate a surprising amount of paperwork. Every client event needs invoices, delivery notes, order confirmations, menu sheets, and tax documentation. When you are running multiple events per week, the document workload adds up fast.

Monthly invoice processing

Each month, I receive a batch of invoices that need formatting, branding updates, and client-specific customisation. My process: maintain templates for each client, batch process all invoices in one session, and double-check every detail before delivery.

PDF delivery notes and documents

Delivery notes need to be clean, professional, and print-ready. I format PDFs with consistent margins, clear headers, and proper branding. For tax documents, I ensure spreadsheets are correctly formatted and calculations are verified.

Image editing for documents

A lot of document work involves images — removing logos from supplier photos, resizing images for menus, preparing visuals for flyers. I handle this as part of the document workflow so everything comes back ready to use.

"From editing our delivery note PDFs to removing logos from images, Jihad does everything we send him quickly and correctly." — S. Alam, Restaurant Director

Why catering businesses outsource this

The main reason is time. An owner spending 8-10 hours a week on documents could be spending that time in the kitchen, with clients, or growing the business.

Why your catering business needs a proper website in 2026

Still taking orders by phone only? Here is what a proper catering website with a booking system can do for your business.

Jihad

Web Developer & Catering Business Support

Phone-only is costing you business

If your catering business still relies on phone calls and text messages to take orders, you are losing potential clients every day. In 2026, people expect to browse a menu, check availability, and enquire online.

What a catering website should have

  • Menu display: Clear, appetising menu pages that are easy to update.
  • Online booking system: Clients can request catering directly through the site.
  • Photo gallery: Showcase your work with a professional gallery.
  • Contact form: Structured enquiries with all info needed upfront.
  • Admin panel: So you can update menus and manage bookings yourself.

The business impact

For Paul Catering House Limited, I built a complete website with booking system and admin panel. Enquiries now come in with all details pre-filled, menus are managed through a simple panel, and the team spends less time on the phone.

"We asked Jihad to build our catering website with a booking system and admin panel. He delivered everything exactly as we described and we now manage it all ourselves." — F. Akhter, Catering Manager

It doesn't have to be expensive

A proper catering website with a booking system and admin panel is achievable on a reasonable budget. One extra booking from the website can cover the cost for months.
